\section{Impact on Artists and Creativity}
\begin{frame}[fragile=singleslide]\frametitle{Algorithmic Influence on Music Creation}
	Artists and labels increasingly craft music to exploit recommendation algorithms.
	Trends in popular music are often driven by what performs well in the recommendation system, potentially limiting creative freedom.
	Music designed for the algorithm is often tailored to broader audiences to gain more streams and revenue.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}\frametitle{Monetization and Streams}
	Streaming income is tied to the number of plays and engagement driven by recommendations.
	Artists may prioritize commercial success over artistic innovation to fit algorithmic preferences.
	Smaller artists can use these algorithms to gain visibility but may face challenges against larger, more commercialized content.
\end{frame}
